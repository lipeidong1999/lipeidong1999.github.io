I am a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at the Innovation Academy for Precision Measurement Science and Technology (APM), Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Mang Feng. I received my B.Sc. in Physics from Wuhan University in 2021. My research focuses on trapped-ion quantum information, quantum thermodynamics, and motional-state engineering. Specifically, I experimentally study single-ion phonon lasers, quantum Szilard engines, and Liouvillian exceptional points. Feel free to explore my [publications](/publications) or contact me at [lipeidong21@mails.ucas.ac.cn](mailto:lipeidong21@mails.ucas.ac.cn).
