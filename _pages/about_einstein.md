I received my B.Sc. in Physics from Wuhan University in 2021. My research focuses on trapped-ion quantum information, quantum thermodynamics, and motional-state engineering. Specifically, I experimentally study single-ion phonon lasers, quantum Szilard engines, and Liouvillian exceptional points.
